# Local voice pipeline rehearsal phrases

English examples are ready to record. Local-language examples are placeholders and need native-speaker review before relying on them in participant-facing flows.

## English

- Registration: “I want to register for the digital skills programme.”
- Sick: “I missed class because I was sick.”
- Recording: “I will watch the recording tomorrow.”
- Transport hardship: “I don’t have money for transport.”
- Human request: “Please let me talk to someone.”

## Nigerian Pidgin — NEEDS_NATIVE_REVIEW

- Registration: “I wan register for the digital skills programme.”
- Sick: “I miss class because I no well.”
- Recording: “I go watch the recording tomorrow.”
- Transport hardship: “I no get money for transport.”
- Human request: “Abeg make I talk to person.”
